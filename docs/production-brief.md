## Proposed editorial structure

Starting point: a 30-45 second landscape software pitch, to be revised for the actual audience and product.

| Beat          | Purpose                          | Direction                                                 |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opening       | Establish the desire or problem  | One concise line; confident visual pause                  |
| Reveal        | Introduce the product and payoff | Real interface with deliberate framing                    |
| Demonstration | Prove the benefit                | One believable workflow; guide the eye through the action |
| Outcome       | Make the result memorable        | Concrete result with a clean visual resolution            |
| Close         | Give one next step               | Product identity and a clear call to action               |

Cut timings should follow the story, interface action, and soundtrack rather than a rigid template. Final length follows the approved script and any recorded narration.

## Quality bar

- Each scene has one unmistakable focal point and enough reading time.
- Typography is consistent, optically spaced, and legible at the intended playback size.
- Interface details are faithful to the product. Data and claims are real or clearly identified as demo content.
- Motion has a purpose: reveal, guide attention, establish hierarchy, or connect actions.
- Cuts land intentionally. Screen movements settle before the viewer needs to read.
- Fonts and media are local or deliberately managed, with provenance recorded.
- Sound reinforces major beats; narration remains intelligible; endings resolve cleanly.
- Check first, middle, and final frames, scene boundaries, text overflow, missing assets, audio clipping, and output dimensions.
